# MyLinkedin Community Code of Conduct

We want **MyLinkedin** to be a welcoming place for people learning, designing, testing, and contributing to backend systems, frontend applications, and open-source software.

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, project discussions, and other official MyLinkedin community spaces. We expect maintainers and contributors to follow it.

## Our standards

Please:

- Be respectful and patient with contributors of all experience levels.
- Share constructive, specific feedback about ideas and code, not personal attacks.
- Welcome questions, carefully reported bugs, thoughtful feature proposals, and alternative design suggestions.
- Give appropriate credit for contributions and acknowledge corrections openly.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and the time of volunteer contributors.
- Keep discussions focused, relevant, and collaborative.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or hateful language.
- Personal insults, deliberate humiliation, or repeated hostile conduct.
- Sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized comments, unwanted advances, or other unwelcome conduct.
- Spam, impersonation, malicious disruption, or persistent off-topic interference.
- Publishing credentials, real user data, or vulnerability details in public conversations.

## Reporting problems

If you experience or observe misconduct, please contact the maintainer **privately** using a contact method made available on the [NimsCreation GitHub profile](https://github.com/nims-creation), if one is provided.

If you cannot find a private contact method, you may open a **neutral public issue requesting a private contact channel**, but **do not include names, allegations, screenshots of private conversations, or sensitive details** in that public issue. Wait until an appropriate private channel is available.

Security vulnerabilities should follow the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md), not ordinary public issue reporting.

Reports will be considered in good faith, with an effort to protect privacy and limit access to relevant information. Confidentiality cannot be absolutely guaranteed, and as a volunteer-maintained project there is no guaranteed response time.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may ask contributors to change their conduct, edit or hide inappropriate content when platform controls permit, close or lock discussions, remove comments, or restrict participation in response to violations.

Actions should be proportionate to the behavior, consider context and prior incidents, and be applied as consistently as practical. Contributors affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration privately through an available maintainer contact method.

## Constructive engineering collaboration

- Discuss significant changes in an issue before opening a large PR.
- Keep criticism specific to code, tests, architecture, and documentation.
- Do not pressure volunteers for immediate reviews or merges.
- Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for development and review expectations.
- Follow the [MIT License](LICENSE) when reusing and distributing code.

Thanks for helping build a respectful and useful developer community around MyLinkedin.
